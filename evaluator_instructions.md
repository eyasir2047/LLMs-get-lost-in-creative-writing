# Story Rating Instructions

Thank you for helping with this study. You will read short stories and give
them scores. Please read this whole page before you start.

## What you will do

You will get a file with many stories. For each story:

1. Read the story fully, start to end.
2. Give it a score from **1 (worst) to 5 (best)** on each of the 6 questions
   below.
3. Write the score in the matching column.

Please do this for **every story** in your file. Do not skip any.

## Rules

- Read each story before scoring it. Do not guess from the title alone.
- Give your own honest opinion. There are no "correct" answers.
- Some stories may be strange, sad, or unfinished. That is expected — just
  rate what you read.
- Do not talk to the other raters about your scores until everyone is done.
- Please don't rush. A careful reading normally takes 1-2 minutes per story.

## The 6 questions (score each one 1 to 5)

### 1. Relevance — Does the story match its title/topic?
- 1 = Story has nothing to do with the topic
- 2 = Story barely connects to the topic
- 3 = Story roughly matches the topic
- 4 = Story matches the topic, with one or two small misses
- 5 = Story matches the topic perfectly

### 2. Coherence — Does the story make sense?
- 1 = Does not make sense at all (characters/setting keep changing randomly)
- 2 = Most of it does not make sense
- 3 = Mostly makes sense, but has some confusing parts
- 4 = Makes sense, with one or two small confusing parts
- 5 = Makes complete sense from start to end

### 3. Empathy — Did you understand how the characters felt?
- 1 = Characters feel empty, no emotion comes through
- 2 = You felt a little connection to one character
- 3 = You clearly noticed feelings (sadness, joy, fear, etc.) in a character
- 4 = You felt emotionally involved with a character, mostly
- 5 = You felt fully emotionally involved with a character

### 4. Surprise — Was the ending surprising?
- 1 = Ending was obvious from the start, or made no sense
- 2 = Ending was easy to guess early on
- 3 = Ending was easy to guess halfway through
- 4 = Ending surprised you, and was hard to guess
- 5 = Ending surprised you, but looking back, the clues were there

### 5. Engagement — Did you enjoy reading it / want to keep reading?
- 1 = Boring, you were glad it was over
- 2 = One or two mildly interesting moments, nothing more
- 3 = Somewhat interesting
- 4 = Almost fully engaging until the end
- 5 = Very engaging, you wished there was more

### 6. Complexity — How rich/detailed is the story?
- 1 = Very simple, just one or two characters or ideas
- 2 = Simple, a basic plot, maybe a time or place mentioned
- 3 = Somewhat developed (has at least one of: rich ideas, believable
      characters, an interesting plot, clear backstory, vivid description)
- 4 = Developed (has at least two of the above)
- 5 = Very developed (has at least three of the above)

## Example

**Title:** "The exterminator's afterlife"

**Story:** "3,000 years have I been fighting. Every morning, the raccoons
scratch at my eyes..."

A rater might score this: Relevance = 5, Coherence = 4, Empathy = 3,
Surprise = 3, Engagement = 4, Complexity = 3.

## Quick checklist before you submit

- [ ] I read every story fully.
- [ ] I gave a score (1-5) for all 6 questions, for every story.
- [ ] I did not skip any rows.

Thank you for your time and careful work!
